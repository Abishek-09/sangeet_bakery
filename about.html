Our Heritage

Rooted in VK Puram, Loved Across Ambasamudram

Located at 982, Ambasamudram - Papanasam - Upper Dam Road, Sangeet Bakery and Sweets was founded on a simple, timeless belief: celebrations deserve real ingredients, genuine craftsmanship, and the aroma of baking fresh from the oven every single day.

What started as a cherished local bakery has grown into a community destination where families gather for evening tea, celebrate birthdays with custom-themed tiered cakes, and share traditional sweets made exclusively with 100% pure ghee during festivals and weddings.

Pure Ingredients

No Shortcuts, Only Uncompromising Quality

We never use synthetic substitutes or compound syrups. Our cakes are made with genuine fresh cream and farm-fresh dairy. Our traditional South Indian sweets are prepared exclusively with 100% pure desi ghee that melts the moment it touches your tongue.

From crispy egg samosas and pepper chicken puffs to our loaded paneer tikka pizzas and burgers, every recipe is tested and perfected for the authentic, comforting taste our patrons expect.
